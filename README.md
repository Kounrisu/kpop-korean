# K-pop and Korean

A public study space for exploring K-pop and learning Korean through music, artists, culture, and language practice.

## Learning goals

- Read Hangul comfortably and build pronunciation skills.
- Learn useful vocabulary and grammar in context.
- Explore artists, groups, music, performance, and the wider Korean music industry.
- Understand cultural references with context rather than relying on stereotypes.
- Keep a dated list of new releases and trustworthy sources.

## Study paths

1. **Korean foundations** — Hangul, pronunciation, sentence order, particles, common endings, and everyday expressions.
2. **Listening practice** — original listening notes; identify vocabulary and grammar, then check against reliable learning materials.
3. **K-pop guide** — artist and group profiles, discographies, concepts, performances, and industry context.
4. **Culture and context** — explain references and terms with sources and room for nuance.
5. **Release notes** — record dates and link to official artist or label announcements.

## Practice notes

Write original summaries and examples. When discussing songs, avoid reproducing full lyrics; use brief excerpts only when needed for language analysis and link to an authorized source.
